For Someone Special

Sometimes I still remember that afternoon light

How you looked at me and everything felt right

Every little thing about you felt so new

I didn’t understand why I kept thinking of you

Like an angel in the middle of my day

And somehow you took my fears away

Maybe it was just a simple crush at first

A feeling quiet, but slowly getting worse

Realizing my heart beats a little too fast

You became someone I hoped would last

Didn’t say much, I just stayed near

Even when I wanted you to hear

Learning chess just to play your game

Only so you might remember my name

Just trying hard in my own small way

Every night thinking what I’d say

Really, you were my reason back then

Over and over, again and again

Couldn’t help but admire you from afar

A presence so gentle, like a distant star

Maybe we never had moments to share

Until now, I’ve only watched from somewhere

Little things about you stayed in my view

Only wishing one day you somehow knew
